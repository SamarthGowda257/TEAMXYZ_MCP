## Direction contract

THESIS: A hospital that reads like a place of care, not a software product. The surface owns
"the ward at first light" — deep teal ink on warm ivory, the calm of a well-run private hospital
before the rush. It refuses the category default this repo already shipped: white page, medical
blue, same-size icon cards, hero-metric strip. Structure comes from ruled bands and one wide
editorial column, not from a grid of equal boxes.

OWN-WORLD: Warm ivory ground (#FBF9F5) under deep forest-teal ink (#0F2E2B), teal (#0D6E63)
carrying actions, muted gold (#C9A227) as the single warm signal, red reserved for 108 alone.
Petrona variable serif for display, Lexend for every UI and body text (chosen for reading
proficiency: this audience is elderly). Hairline rules and tinted bands instead of shadows;
pill controls; tabular numerals everywhere a fee, time or booking ID appears.

STORY: A patient (or their son, in another city) understands within one viewport that this
hospital answers in their own words, day or night, and that a real person is one sentence away.
They book, or they open the chat.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed ivory. Left column two-thirds: a Petrona headline at display scale,
one sentence of Lexend beneath, then two controls — solid teal "Book a visit" and an outlined
"Call" carrying the real Talkdesk number. Right third: a single photograph of an Indian hospital
interior, full-bleed to the right edge, overlaid at its lower-left by one live fact card drawn
from the API (next free cardiology slot), not a fake chat transcript. A hairline gold rule runs
under the whole viewport. Emergency 108 sits above the header, always.

FORM: Editorial ruled band system, ranked 1 of 7 derived candidates. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the
verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

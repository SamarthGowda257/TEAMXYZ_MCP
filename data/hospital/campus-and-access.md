## OPD timings and OPD hours
Our OPD timings are Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 8 PM. Individual doctors consult only on their own days and hours, so please check the doctor's schedule when you book. Emergency, the pharmacy and the laboratory are open 24 hours, every day including Sunday.

## Hospital size and capacity
WellNest Hospitals is a 250-bed multi-speciality hospital on a 3.8 acre campus at Marathahalli, Bengaluru. We have 45 intensive care beds, 15 daycare surgery beds, and 32 consultation chambers that see 1,200 to 1,500 outpatients a day. The hospital is accredited by NABH, and our laboratory is NABL accredited to ISO 15189.

## Gate 1, the emergency entrance
Gate 1 on the west wing is the 24-hour emergency and trauma entrance, also called entrance B. Ambulances dock directly there and it has a zero-grade ramp with no steps. A porter station is staffed there around the clock.

## Gate 2, the main entrance
Gate 2 on the north wing is the main hospital entrance and the OPD atrium. Use it for outpatient clinics, diagnostic imaging and the visitor lobby. It has automated doors, the concierge reception, the wheelchair desk, a coffee kiosk and valet drop-off.

## Gate 3, parking and deliveries
Gate 3 on the east wing is the entry ramp for the basement car parks P1 and P2. It is also used for pharmacy deliveries and hospital services, so please do not use it as a patient entrance.

## Gate 4, staff entrance
Gate 4 on the south wing is the staff entrance with biometric turnstiles, and it also gives access to the auditorium. Patients and visitors should use Gate 1 or Gate 2.

## Which floor is my department on
Cardiology, Neurology, Gastroenterology and Pulmonology are on the third floor. Orthopaedics, Nephrology, the dialysis centre and Endocrinology are on the fourth floor. Dermatology, ENT, Ophthalmology, Paediatrics and the women and child centre are on the second floor.

## Ground floor and basement
The ground floor has the main atrium, central reception, the 24-hour emergency at entrance B, the 24-hour pharmacy, the cafeteria and the ATM. Basement 1 has diagnostic imaging and the pathology laboratory. Basement 2 is patient parking.

## First floor
The first floor has the executive health check-up lounge, the blood bank, the multifaith prayer room, hospital administration and the insurance and TPA billing counters.

## Operating theatres
The surgical complex is on the fifth floor with six modular operating theatres and a recovery area. There is a dedicated cardiac theatre, a robotic and laparoscopy suite, an orthopaedic and spine theatre, a neurosurgery theatre, a general and daycare theatre, and an obstetrics theatre for emergency caesarean sections.

## Cardiac catheterisation laboratory
The cardiac cath lab is on the fifth floor and runs 24 hours a day, every day of the year. It is used for emergency angioplasty, electrophysiology studies and pacemaker implantation. For a major heart attack the on-call team mobilises within 20 minutes and aims to open the blocked artery within 60 minutes.

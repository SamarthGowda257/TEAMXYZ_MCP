# WellNest hospital policies

## Free follow-up review
Every paid outpatient consultation includes one free follow-up review with the same doctor for the same problem within 7 calendar days. From day 8 it is a fresh consultation.

## Reporting time and vitals
Please report at the floor reception 15 minutes before your slot, so a nurse can record your blood pressure, pulse, oxygen level, temperature and weight.

## New patient registration charge
New patients pay a one-time 150 rupee charge for their lifetime digital health record, called the UHID.

## Wheelchair and porter escort
Wheelchair and porter help is free for all patients on hospital grounds. It is a two-way escort: the porter meets you at the gate, takes you to the clinic, and takes you back to your vehicle after the visit. Please ask 15 minutes ahead, and show your token, which starts with A S, at the porter desk.

## Taking a wheelchair off campus
Wheelchairs normally stay on hospital grounds. If an exception is approved, it needs a 1,000 rupee refundable deposit and a security gate pass. The deposit comes back when the wheelchair is returned to Gate 2.

## Cashless insurance approval
Checking coverage by phone or chat is only an estimate, not a guarantee. The insurer gives the final approval. Please bring your insurance card, a government photo ID and your doctor's written request to the TPA desk on the ground floor, Wing B, at least 2 hours before a major scan.

## Costs insurance does not cover
Insurers do not pay for non-medical items such as file charges, protective kits and disposable syringes. These usually come to about 850 rupees and are paid at the counter.

## Tax receipts
Payment receipts for consultations, tests and hospital stays carry the hospital's PAN and GSTIN and can be used for a Section 80D claim.

## Lab report timing
A blood count usually takes 2 to 3 hours, and liver, kidney, lipid and thyroid tests about 4 hours. Blood cultures take 48 to 72 hours and biopsy results 3 to 5 working days. Stamped printed copies of scans are at the diagnostic desk in Basement 1.

## Who can receive reports
Reports go only to the patient's registered email and the patient portal. We can't send them to a family member's or a work email address. A family member needs a signed medical proxy form at the front desk.

## What a caregiver can see
A registered caregiver gets practical updates: appointment details, porter tokens, doctor delays and delivery times, by email, SMS and phone. Lab results and doctor's notes stay private unless the patient signs a medical proxy form.

## Medicine home delivery
Our 24 hour pharmacy delivers within 90 minutes in Marathahalli, Kadubeesanahalli, Munnekollal, Brookefield and Bellandur, and the same day in Whitefield, HSR Layout, Koramangala and Indiranagar. Delivery is free from 500 rupees, otherwise 50 rupees.

## Medicines that need a prescription
Medicines without a schedule can be ordered freely. Schedule H and H1 medicines, including antibiotics, need a doctor's prescription from the last 6 months on your record. Schedule X medicines such as sedatives can't be ordered by chat or phone at all and need an in-person consultation.

## Cold-chain medicines
Insulin and other medicines kept at 2 to 8 degrees travel in an insulated pouch. An adult must be home to receive them with an OTP and put them in the fridge straight away. We can't leave them at a gate or doorstep.

## Cancelling a medicine order
You can cancel a medicine order until it leaves the pharmacy, and your credits come back. Once it has been dispatched it can't be recalled, for drug safety. If you refuse it at the door, the pharmacy audit team checks the sealed package before any partial refund.

## What HealthCredits are
HealthCredits are a prepaid WellNest wallet, where 1 credit equals 1 rupee. They pay for medicines, consultations, tests, bills and wheelchair deposits at WellNest only, and can't be transferred to anyone or spent elsewhere.

## Adding HealthCredits
Adding 2,000 rupees gives 2,100 credits, a 5 percent bonus. Adding 5,000 rupees gives 5,350 credits, a 7 percent bonus. We send a secure payment link and never take card details by chat or phone.

## HealthCredits refunds
Under RBI wallet rules, credits can't be paid out as cash. Unused credits are refunded to the bank account or card you paid from, usually within 3 to 5 banking days.

## Sharing HealthCredits with family
A wallet owner can let a registered family member pay from their credits, with a limit per order or per day. The owner gets an SMS every time credits are used, and can stop it at any time.

## HealthCredits on a child's account
A child's credits can only be spent with a parent or guardian's approval, so the payment link goes to the guardian.

## Parking charges
Outpatients park free for the first 3 hours with a valid consultation slip, then 20 rupees an hour. Inpatient attendants get a pass at 100 rupees a day. Other visitors pay 30 rupees for the first 2 hours, then 20 rupees an hour. Valet at the main entrance costs 50 rupees plus the parking fee.

## Electric vehicle charging
We have AC chargers at 14 rupees per unit and DC fast chargers at 19 rupees per unit in the basement car park.

## Room charges
A general ward bed is 2,200 rupees a day, a twin-sharing room 4,500 rupees, a single deluxe private room 7,800 rupees, an executive suite 16,500 rupees, and an ICU bed 11,500 rupees.

## Complaint response times
Every complaint gets a tracking number starting with G R V. Urgent clinical concerns get a call back within 15 minutes, other requests for a person within 2 hours, and billing reviews within 24 hours. You can also reach patient relations on 080 4000 1290.

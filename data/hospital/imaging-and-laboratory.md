## Where imaging and the laboratory are
Diagnostic imaging and the pathology laboratory are both in Basement 1. The imaging rooms are lead-shielded to the Atomic Energy Regulatory Board's safety standards. You can collect stamped hard copies of any report from the diagnostic reception in Basement 1.

## Imaging equipment
We have two 3 tesla MRI scanners, two 128-slice CT scanners, four digital X-ray rooms and six high-resolution ultrasound machines. Reports are read by our own radiologists, who consult in Basement 1 from Monday to Saturday.

## MRI scan prices
An MRI of the brain is 8,500 rupees and takes about 45 minutes. An MRI of the spine is 9,500 rupees and an MRI of the knee is 7,500 rupees. None of the MRI scans need fasting, but you must tell us about any pacemaker, metal implant or surgical clip before the scan.

## MRI safety and preparation
Before an MRI we screen you for a cardiac pacemaker, metal implants or aneurysm clips, because the magnet is always on. Please arrive 30 minutes early, change into a metal-free gown and leave jewellery at home. Tell the technician if you are claustrophobic, and earplugs are provided because the scanner is loud.

## CT scan prices
A CT scan of the head is 3,500 rupees, a high-resolution CT of the chest is 5,500 rupees, and a CT of the abdomen and pelvis is 6,500 rupees. The chest and abdomen scans need four hours of fasting when contrast is used, and a recent kidney function report is required before any contrast scan.

## Ultrasound prices and preparation
An ultrasound of the whole abdomen is 1,600 rupees and needs six hours of fasting; drink a litre of water an hour before and come with a full bladder. An ultrasound of the pelvis is 1,500 rupees, needs no fasting, but also needs a full bladder, so drink four or five glasses of water 45 minutes before.

## X-ray prices
A digital chest X-ray is 450 rupees and a knee X-ray is 500 rupees, and both take about 10 minutes with no preparation. Please remove necklaces, metal chains and any underwired garment before the X-ray.

## Blood test turnaround times
A complete blood count is ready in two to three hours, and an urgent sample for dengue or trauma in about 45 minutes. A lipid profile, liver function test or thyroid profile takes about four hours, and a kidney function test three to four hours. A urine routine test is ready in two hours.

## Blood culture and biopsy turnaround
A blood culture with antibiotic sensitivity takes 48 to 72 hours, with an early alert at 24 hours if it turns positive. A biopsy or histopathology report takes three to five working days because the tissue has to be processed and stained.

## Fasting for blood tests
A lipid profile needs 10 to 12 hours of overnight fasting, and water is allowed. Fasting blood sugar needs eight hours and a liver function test about four hours, and you should avoid alcohol for 24 hours before a liver test. A complete blood count, HbA1c, kidney function test and thyroid test can be taken without fasting, though a morning sample is preferred for thyroid.

## Kidney function test before contrast
A kidney function test is compulsory before any CT scan with contrast and before certain strong medicines, because contrast can affect the kidneys. Please bring the report with you or have the test done here at least a few hours before the scan.

## Emergency department
Our emergency and trauma centre is open 24 hours at Gate 1, entrance B, on the ground floor. It has 12 resuscitation and triage beds, two isolation bays and a minor operating theatre. For a life-threatening emergency call 108 or come straight in; do not wait for an appointment.

## How emergency patients are prioritised
Emergency patients are seen in order of how serious their condition is, not in order of arrival. Cardiac arrest, major trauma, a stroke or a blocked airway is seen by a doctor immediately. Chest discomfort with stable vitals, severe asthma or an open fracture is seen within 15 minutes, and minor injuries, sprains or a low fever within 45 minutes.

## Ambulance service
We run three advanced life support ambulances with ventilators, defibrillators, infusion pumps and on-board ECG telemetry, plus one basic life support van for non-critical transfers. To call an ambulance for the Marathahalli, Whitefield or Bellandur area, dial our dispatch line on 080 4000 108.

## Emergency payment
Emergency treatment is never delayed for payment or paperwork. Registration and billing are completed after the patient is stable. Medico-legal cases are reported to the police as the law requires.

## Intensive care units
We have 45 critical care beds in glass-partitioned cubicles. There are 20 medical and surgical intensive care beds and 10 coronary care beds on the third floor, and 10 neonatal intensive care beds and 5 paediatric intensive care beds on the second floor. Ventilated patients have one nurse each.

## Neonatal intensive care
Our neonatal unit is a level three NICU on the second floor with high-frequency ventilators, phototherapy, total parenteral nutrition and transport incubators for newborns who need to be moved.

## ICU visiting rules
ICU visiting is strictly 5 PM to 6 PM every day, and only one adult family member may go into the cubicle at a time. Hand hygiene and shoe covers are compulsory. Children under 12 are not allowed into the ICU at any time.

## Dialysis
The dialysis centre has 20 stations on the fourth floor next to the nephrology clinic. We provide haemodialysis and peritoneal dialysis, and we assess patients for kidney transplant.

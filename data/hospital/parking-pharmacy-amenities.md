## Parking charges
Outpatients park free for the first three hours; get the barcode on your consultation slip scanned at the billing counter, and after three hours it is 20 rupees an hour. General visitors pay 30 rupees for the first two hours and 20 rupees an hour after that. An inpatient attendant gets one vehicle pass at a flat 100 rupees a day with unlimited entry and exit.

## Where to park
Patient and visitor parking is in the basement, entered from Gate 3. Level P1 has 120 car bays kept for outpatients and senior citizens plus 200 two-wheeler bays, and level P2 has 160 car bays and 250 two-wheeler bays. Twelve extra-wide accessible bays are on P1 within 15 metres of the lifts.

## Valet parking
Valet parking is available only at Gate 2, the main atrium, from 8 AM to 8 PM. It costs 50 rupees on top of the normal parking charge.

## Electric vehicle charging
There is an EV charging hub on basement level P1 with four slower type-2 chargers at 14 rupees a unit and four fast DC chargers at 19 rupees a unit. A fast charger takes a car from 20 percent to 80 percent in about 35 minutes.

## Pharmacy hours and location
The main pharmacy is on the ground floor next to Gate 1 and is open 24 hours a day, every day of the year. There is a second dispensary on the fourth floor that serves the wards and operating theatres. We stock over 1,200 medicines.

## Medicine home delivery
We deliver prescriptions free within five kilometres of the hospital, which covers Marathahalli, Kadubeesanahalli, Munnekollal and Brookefield, for orders above 500 rupees. To arrange a delivery call the pharmacy desk on 080 4000 1240.

## Repeat prescriptions for long-term medicines
We track 30-day supplies of regular medicines for blood pressure, cholesterol, diabetes and asthma, and we send you or your caregiver a reminder four days before the supply runs out. You can then ask us to deliver the refill or to book a review with your doctor.

## Antibiotics and restricted medicines
Antibiotics, sleep medicines and strong painkillers are prescription-only under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, and we cannot dispense them without a valid prescription signed by a doctor within the last six months. We cannot approve these over chat or on the phone; you need a consultation first.

## Cafeteria
The cafeteria is on the ground floor in the south wing and is open every day from 7 AM to 10 PM. It is a fully vegetarian kitchen serving South Indian and North Indian food, fresh juices and diabetic-friendly meals. An idli-vada combination is about 60 rupees and a mini thali about 120 rupees.

## Food for inpatients
Ward patients get meals from our own kitchen, planned by the clinical nutritionist according to what the doctor has advised, including salt-restricted, diabetic, kidney and liquid diets. Meals come to the bedside in heated trolleys. Outside food for a patient needs the nurse's or dietitian's approval.

## Other facilities on the campus
There is a coffee kiosk in the Gate 2 lobby open from 7 AM to 11 PM, a 24-hour ATM in the main reception corridor, and a multifaith prayer and meditation room on the first floor open at all hours. A private nursing and baby-changing room is on the first floor next to the children's clinic.

## Smoking, alcohol and photography
The whole campus, including the car parks, is a no smoking, no tobacco and no alcohol area. Please do not photograph or film other patients or staff. Keep mobile phones on silent in the wards and switched off in the ICU and operating areas.

## Free follow-up review within 7 days
Your consultation fee covers today's visit and one free follow-up review within seven calendar days, for the same complaint. The review visit carries no consultation fee. From the eighth day onwards it is charged as a fresh consultation.

## Reporting time and vitals
Please reach the floor reception 15 minutes before your appointment. The floor nurse records your blood pressure, pulse, oxygen level, temperature and weight before you see the doctor, and this is why the early arrival matters.

## If you arrive late
Your slot is held for 15 minutes past the appointment time. If you are later than that, we will usually fit you in at the end of that clinic session as a walk-in rather than cancel you, so that the patients booked after you are not delayed. Tell us if you are stuck in traffic and we will note it.

## New patient registration charge
A patient who is new to WellNest pays a one-time registration charge of 150 rupees for a lifetime digital health record. This is charged once only; every later visit uses the same record, and you can ask for your record number at the front desk.

## When a doctor is fully booked
If the doctor you want has no free slots, you can join the waitlist for that doctor. When somebody cancels, we send an offer to the next person on the list by message and email, and the offer is held for a short time so you need to accept it promptly. You can also book a different doctor in the same department straight away.

## Patient rights
You have the right to respectful care, to be told your diagnosis and treatment choices in a language you understand, to a second opinion, to an itemised bill, to privacy, and to raise a complaint without it affecting your care.

## Raising a complaint
You can raise a complaint on chat, on the phone, by email to care@wellnest.test, or with the patient relations officer at the front desk. Every complaint gets a tracking reference. We acknowledge it within 24 hours and aim to resolve it within seven working days.

## How quickly a complaint is answered
A clinical or urgent concern passed to a person is picked up within about 15 minutes during working hours. A billing dispute that needs an account audit is answered within 24 hours. For an escalation you can also email grievance@wellnest.test or call 080 4000 1290.

## Consent for procedures
Any procedure or surgery needs your written informed consent after the doctor has explained the benefits and the risks. For a child or a patient who cannot decide for themselves, a parent or legal guardian signs the consent.

## Children and young patients
A patient under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian, who gives consent for treatment. Children are welcome in the general wards during visiting hours, but children under 12 are not allowed into the ICU.

## Second opinion
You can ask for a second opinion from another consultant at any point, and asking for one will not affect the care you receive. Tell the front desk or our assistant and we will book you with another specialist in the same department and share your existing reports with them.

## Cancelling or rescheduling an appointment
You can cancel or reschedule an outpatient booking free of charge, on chat, on the phone or in the patient portal, and there is no cancellation charge. If you prepaid the consultation fee it is returned to the same payment method within five to seven working days. A health check package cancelled less than 24 hours before is refunded after a ten percent fee.

## What happens to the slot you give up
When you cancel, the slot is offered straight away to the next patient waiting for that doctor, so the appointment is not wasted. If you are rescheduling rather than cancelling, tell us you want a different time and we will find the next free slot with the same doctor instead of cancelling your booking.

## Insurance and TPA desk
The insurance and TPA desk is on the ground floor in wing B, at counters 12 to 16, open Monday to Saturday from 8 AM to 7 PM. Outside those hours the duty billing manager handles emergency admissions. For a planned scan please come to the desk about two hours before your appointment.

## Which insurers we accept for cashless treatment
We are empanelled for cashless treatment with Star Health, HDFC ERGO, Care Health Insurance, ICICI Lombard, Niva Bupa, Bajaj Allianz, Tata AIG and SBI General. We also work with the third-party administrators Medi Assist, Vidal Health, Heritage Health, Paramount, Family Health Plan, Raksha and MDIndia.

## Government health schemes
We accept Ayushman Bharat, the PM-JAY scheme, and the Karnataka Arogya Sanjeevini scheme for emergency cardiac and trauma admissions, subject to the scheme portal approving the case. Bring the scheme card and a government photo identity document.

## What a coverage check on chat or phone actually means
When we check your insurance on chat or on the phone we are confirming that the hospital is in your insurer's network and giving you an estimate. It is not a guarantee of payment. The final cashless approval comes from your insurer's medical team after they see the doctor's notes and your investigations.

## Documents to bring for cashless treatment
Bring your original insurance card or e-card showing the policy number, a government photo identity document such as an Aadhaar card or passport, and the doctor's written requisition or admission advice. Without all three the desk cannot start the cashless request.

## How long pre-authorisation takes
For an emergency admission the insurer usually gives a first decision within one to two hours of the documents being uploaded. For a planned surgery or daycare procedure the request should reach us at least 48 hours in advance so the approval is ready before you arrive.

## Items insurance does not pay for
Insurance regulations exclude non-medical consumables, so these are paid by you before discharge. They include the admission file charge of about 250 rupees, protective and sanitisation kits, disposable bedsheets, gloves and contrast syringes, which together usually come to about 600 to 850 rupees.

## Tax receipts under section 80D
Every receipt we issue for a consultation, a laboratory test or a hospital stay carries the hospital's PAN and GST numbers, so it can be used to claim a deduction under section 80D of the Income Tax Act. Ask the billing counter for a consolidated statement if you need one for the whole year.

## Paying the final bill
The final bill is settled at discharge, after the doctor's review and the pharmacy returns are adjusted. If your treatment is cashless you pay only the excluded items and anything above your policy limit. We accept cards, UPI and net banking, and digital receipts are emailed to you.

## Getting a cost estimate before treatment
Ask the billing desk or our assistant for an estimate before a planned procedure, and we will give it in writing with the room rent, the surgeon's and anaesthetist's fees, the theatre charge and the likely consumables. The estimate is based on an uncomplicated stay, so it can change if you need extra days or extra care.

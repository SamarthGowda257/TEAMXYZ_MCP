## Room types and daily room rent
A bed in the general ward costs 2,200 rupees a day, a twin-sharing room 4,500 rupees a day, and a single deluxe private room 7,800 rupees a day. The executive suite is 16,500 rupees a day and an ICU or coronary care bed is 11,500 rupees a day. Every room rate includes 24-hour nursing, the duty doctor's rounds and housekeeping.

## General ward
The general ward is a six-bed room with central air-conditioning, privacy curtains and a shared bathroom, at 2,200 rupees a day. One attendant pass is issued, which gives a bedside chair and access to the common waiting lounge.

## Twin sharing room
A twin-sharing room has two beds separated by a soundproof curtain, with a television, personal storage and an attached bathroom, at 4,500 rupees a day. One attendant pass is issued with an overnight reclining chair.

## Single private room
The single deluxe private room is 7,800 rupees a day. It has a motorised hospital bed, a flat-screen television, a sofa that converts to a bed, a private bathroom, a refrigerator and Wi-Fi. One attendant may stay overnight on the sleeper sofa.

## Executive suite
The executive suite is 16,500 rupees a day. It is a two-room suite with a separate living lounge for visitors, a kitchenette with a microwave, two televisions, a desk and a dining table. Up to two attendants are permitted.

## ICU bed charges
An intensive care or coronary care bed is 11,500 rupees a day and includes full invasive monitoring, a high-dependency bed and one dedicated nurse. Attendants cannot stay at the bedside in the ICU but may use the waiting lounge.

## Admission deposit
A planned admission needs an advance deposit, and the amount depends on the procedure and the room you choose. The billing desk gives you a written estimate before admission. If your insurance is cashless you pay only the items your policy does not cover.

## Changing rooms during a stay
You can ask the nursing station to move you to a different room type if one is free, and the rate changes from the day of the move. If you move to a room above the limit your insurance allows, the difference and any linked charges are settled by you at discharge.

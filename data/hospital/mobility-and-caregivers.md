## Wheelchair and porter help
Wheelchair help with a porter is completely free anywhere on the hospital campus, for every outpatient. We keep 40 wheelchairs and 12 stretchers. Tell us which gate you will arrive at and roughly when, and a porter will be waiting for you.

## Two-way escort, the return trip
Our porter escort works both ways. The porter meets you at the entrance and takes you to the consulting room, and when your visit finishes the clinic nurse pages the porter desk so someone takes you back down to your car or taxi. You do not have to arrange the return trip yourself.

## Porter desks and timings
The porter desk at Gate 1, the emergency bay, is staffed 24 hours and keeps 15 wheelchairs and 8 stretchers. The desk at Gate 2, the main OPD atrium, works from 7:30 AM to 8:30 PM with 20 wheelchairs. There are also five wheelchairs at the P1 basement lift lobby for people coming straight from their car.

## Booking wheelchair help in advance
Please tell us at least 15 minutes before you arrive so a porter is free when you reach the gate. When the request is booked you get a service token beginning with the letters A and S, and you show that token at the porter desk of the gate you chose.

## Taking a wheelchair off the campus
Wheelchairs may not leave the hospital grounds, so they cannot be taken to a hotel or home. If the hospital superintendent grants an exception, a refundable deposit of 1,000 rupees and a security gate pass are needed, and the deposit is returned when the wheelchair comes back.

## Stretcher and ambulance transfer at the gate
Stretchers with oxygen brackets and drip stands are available at both patient gates if someone cannot sit in a wheelchair. If a patient arrives by ambulance, the Gate 1 team meets the vehicle at the dock, so you do not need to book anything in advance.

## Adding a caregiver to your record
You can add a family member as a registered caregiver, such as an adult son or daughter for an elderly parent. We need the caregiver's name, mobile number and email, and your spoken consent. The caregiver then receives booking confirmations, appointment tokens, reminders and alerts if the doctor is delayed.

## What a caregiver can and cannot see
A registered caregiver receives operational updates only: appointment tokens, clinic room directions, porter status, reminders and schedule changes, by email, message and phone call. Lab reports, scan results and the doctor's clinical notes stay private to the patient under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, unless the patient signs a medical proxy form at the front desk.

## Help for senior citizens
Senior patients are offered free wheelchair help and a porter escort when they book, and they can be seen in the senior citizen wellness clinic with our geriatric physician. Twelve extra-wide accessible parking bays sit within 15 metres of the lifts on basement level P1.

## Interpreter and language help
Our staff and doctors between them speak English, Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Marathi, Urdu and Konkani. Tell us the language you are most comfortable in and we will note it on your record and try to book a doctor who speaks it.
